# PhD Dissertation Context

A doctoral dissertation must make a defensible, original contribution to knowledge with high methodological and theoretical rigor.

## Type Selection

- **Type A (Empirical/Scientific):** Original empirical investigation.
- **Type B (Theoretical/Philosophical):** Conceptual/theoretical advancement.
- **Type C (Humanities/Interpretive):** Interpretive analysis of texts/artifacts/history.
- **Type D (Practice-Based):** Creative/practice output with scholarly exegesis.

## Doctoral-Level Expectations

- Explicit and substantial contribution claim.
- Comprehensive command of relevant scholarship.
- Strong epistemological/theoretical-methodological coherence.
- Clear scope boundaries and reflexive awareness of trade-offs.

## Introduction Standard (CARS + Positioning)

- Establish territory and importance.
- Define precise niche/gap.
- Occupy niche with research questions/thesis and contribution.
- Briefly position epistemological stance where relevant.

## Literature Review Standard

- Demonstrate depth and breadth across major debates and traditions.
- Critically evaluate methods and assumptions in prior work.
- Show unresolved tensions your dissertation addresses.

## Framework and Method Expectations

- State and justify theoretical/conceptual framework.
- Ensure methodology fits questions and epistemic stance.
- Detail rigor strategy (validity/trustworthiness/justification as relevant by discipline).

## Contribution Requirement

Provide a direct contribution statement that identifies what is new:
- Theoretical, methodological, empirical, and/or practical contribution.

## Citation and Integrity Rules

- Anchor substantive claims to evidence.
- Do not fabricate citations, statistics, or findings.
- Use qualitative phrasing when numeric detail is unavailable.

## Optional Table Patterns

- Comprehensive study/framework comparison tables.
- Results/theme/robustness summary tables.
- Source-analysis tables for interpretive dissertations.
