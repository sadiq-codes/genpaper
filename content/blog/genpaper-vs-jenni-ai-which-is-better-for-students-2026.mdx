## Citation Accuracy: The Real Test

Citation accuracy isn't just a feature—it's the foundation of academic integrity. Here's why this matters so much.

### The Fake Citation Problem

AI language models are trained to generate plausible-sounding text. When asked for citations, they often create references that:

- Have real author names but fake paper titles
- Cite journals that exist but articles that don't
- Include DOIs that lead nowhere
- Reference correct years but wrong publications

Professors are increasingly checking citations. Many use tools that verify whether sources actually exist. Getting caught with fake citations can result in:

- Automatic failing grades
- Academic integrity violations
- Potential suspension

### How GenPaper Solves This

GenPaper's citation system works differently:

1. You write a claim or point that needs support
2. GenPaper searches real academic databases
3. It finds actual papers that support your point
4. You choose which sources to cite
5. Citations are formatted automatically with correct metadata

Every citation can be verified. Every source actually exists. This is the fundamental difference for students who need their work to hold up to scrutiny.
