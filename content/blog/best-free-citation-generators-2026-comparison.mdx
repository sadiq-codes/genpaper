## What Is a Citation Generator?

A citation generator is a tool that automatically formats your sources into proper citations. You input information about a book, article, or website, and it outputs a correctly formatted citation in APA, MLA, Chicago, or other styles.

**Why use one?**

- **Save time**: Format citations in seconds instead of minutes
- **Reduce errors**: Avoid common formatting mistakes
- **Stay consistent**: Maintain uniform style throughout your paper
- **Learn proper formatting**: See how citations should look

However, not all citation generators are created equal. Some produce inaccurate results, and even the best ones require you to double-check their output.

## Common Citation Generator Mistakes to Avoid

Even the best citation generators can produce errors. Here's how to avoid common problems:

### 1. Trusting Output Blindly

**The problem:** Citation generators sometimes miss details or format incorrectly.

**The fix:** Always compare output against your style guide's official examples. Check author names, dates, and italics especially.

### 2. Using the Wrong Edition

**The problem:** APA 6th vs 7th edition have significant differences. Same with MLA 8 vs 9.

**The fix:** Confirm which edition your professor requires before generating citations.

### 3. Incomplete Source Information

**The problem:** If you enter incomplete data, you get incomplete citations.

**The fix:** Gather full source details before using the generator. For journal articles, get DOI, volume, issue, and page numbers.

### 4. Forgetting In-Text Citations

**The problem:** Generating a bibliography but not matching in-text citations.

**The fix:** Use tools that generate both formats together, or manually ensure they match.

### 5. Not Alphabetizing

**The problem:** Some generators list sources in entry order, not alphabetical order.

**The fix:** Check that your final bibliography is properly alphabetized (APA, MLA) or numbered (some Chicago styles).

## FAQ

### Are free citation generators accurate?

Most free citation generators are **accurate enough** for basic sources like books and journal articles. However, they often make errors with unusual source types (podcasts, social media, government documents). Always double-check against your style guide.

### Which citation generator is best for APA?

**Scribbr** is the most accurate free option specifically for APA 7th edition. GenPaper is also highly accurate and integrates citations into paper writing.

### Can I trust Citation Machine?

Citation Machine works for basic citations but has accuracy issues with APA 7th edition formatting. We recommend using Scribbr or MyBib instead, or double-checking all Citation Machine output carefully.

### Do I still need to check citations manually?

**Yes, always.** Even the best citation generators make occasional errors. Spend 2-3 minutes verifying each citation against official style guide examples before submitting your paper.
