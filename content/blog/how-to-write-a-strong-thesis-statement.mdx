# How to Write a Strong Thesis Statement

A thesis statement is the backbone of any research paper.

## What is a Thesis Statement?

A thesis statement is a one or two-sentence summary of the main point of your paper.

## Key Elements

1. **Specific**: Avoid vague language
2. **Arguable**: Present a claim that can be debated
3. **Focused**: Address one main idea
